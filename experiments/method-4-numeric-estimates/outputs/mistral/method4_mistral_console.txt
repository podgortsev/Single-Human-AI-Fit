====================================================================================
COHERENCE CHECK
====================================================================================
SUCCESS asks whether the person does well. CHURN, DAYS and SUPPORT ask
the opposite kind of question. A description that raises the first and
also raises the others is not being tracked coherently, and its numbers
mean nothing however small the p values are.
This is the same logic that caught a broken instrument in the earlier
work, where a model rated the same writer more intelligent AND more
stupid at once.

  SUCCESS higher is better for the person. CHURN, DAYS and SUPPORT higher is
  worse. When SUCCESS and one of those move the same way, the model is saying
  the person is both more likely to do well and more likely to leave, or take
  longer, at the same time. That is not a judgement about the person, and its
  p value does not rescue it.
